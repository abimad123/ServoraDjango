Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know about Servora's marketplace and booking workflow.

How does Servora work?
Servora is a two-sided marketplace. Homeowners search for local services, compare verified providers based on transparent reviews and pricing, and book a suitable date and time slot directly on the platform.
How are service providers verified?
Providers undergo credential checks, skill verifications, and background identification. Verified partners receive a blue verification badge on their profile cards.
Can I cancel or reschedule a booking?
Yes, customers can view their active bookings under the "My Bookings" page and request cancellation or rescheduling before the provider arrives.
How are prices determined?
Each service provider sets transparent starting prices for their offerings. Final charges correspond directly to the scope agreed upon during the booking submission.
How do I become a service provider?
Click on "Become a Provider" in the top navigation, register your provider account, list your services with pricing, and immediately start receiving local booking requests.
