# Servora — RGM Revenue Pilot & Verifiable Revenue Tracking Guide

**Project:** Servora – Local Home Services Marketplace  
**Framework:** Django 5.x + SQLite + Django ORM  
**Target Category:** University Student-Centric Revenue Generation Model (SCRGM) — Self-Employed Category  
**Pilot Target Bracket:** ₹10,000 – ₹24,999  

---

## 1. Servora Business Model

Servora is a hyperlocal technology marketplace connecting residential homeowners ("Customers") with local skilled service professionals ("Providers"). 

Servora operates on an asset-light, multi-sided platform monetization model. Rather than employing service workers directly or taking custody of customer inventory, Servora monetizes market activity through three distinct channels:
1. **Performance-Based Platform Transaction Fees (10% Booking Commission)**
2. **Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) Provider Subscriptions (₹399/month Servora Pro)**
3. **Targeted In-Platform Advertising / Visibility Boosts (₹99 for 3-Day Featured Listings)**

## 3. Gross Booking Value (GBV) vs. Platform Revenue vs. Provider Earnings

A fundamental tenet of marketplace accounting and university SCRGM compliance is the strict mathematical distinction between Gross Booking Value, Servora Earned Revenue, and Provider Earnings:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              GROSS BOOKING VALUE (GBV)                   │
│              Total Customer Expenditure                   │
│                       (e.g., ₹5,000)                      │
└─────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                              │
              ┌───────────────┴───────────────┐
              ▼                               ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐   ┌───────────────────────────┐
│  SERVORA PLATFORM REVENUE │   │     PROVIDER EARNINGS     │
│   10% Platform Commission │   │  90% Direct Service Labor │
│           (₹500)          │   │          (₹4,500)         │
└───────────────────────────┘   └───────────────────────────┘
```

- **Gross Booking Value (GBV):** The gross transaction value flowing through the marketplace. For a ₹5,000 plumbing job, GBV is ₹5,000. Servora **CANNOT** claim ₹5,000 as student revenue.
- **Servora Platform Revenue:** Only the 10% commission fee (₹500) constitutes earned platform revenue eligible for SCRGM credit.
- **Provider Earnings:** The remaining 90% (₹4,500) belongs entirely to the independent service provider for labor and materials.

## 4. Genuine Transaction Workflow

Every transaction submitted for actual SCRGM revenue credit must follow this real-world operational lifecycle:

```text
1. DISCOVERY: Customer finds a genuine local service on Servora.
       ↓
2. BOOKING: Customer selects scheduled date/time and submits address.
       ↓
3. CONFIRMATION: Provider accepts booking request.
       ↓
4. SERVICE DELIVERY: Provider physically performs the requested task.
       ↓
5. ACTUAL PAYMENT: Customer transfers funds electronically (UPI/IMPS/NEFT).
       ↓
6. COMPLETION: Provider marks booking "Completed" in provider portal.
       ↓
7. COMMISSION SETTLED: System generates RevenueTransaction (status=completed).
       ↓
8. RECEIPT & REVIEW: Commercial service receipt generated; customer reviews.
       ↓
9. AUDIT EVIDENCE: Bank statement UTR and transaction reference archived.
```

### Acceptable Student Pilot Participation:
Friends, family, and neighbors may participate as customers or providers if and only if:
- They genuinely require the service performed.
- The service provider physically and satisfactorily executes the work.
- Real electronic payment actually takes place and is settled through banking channels.
- The transaction is non-circular (no money returned under the table).

## 5. Required Evidence for University SCRGM Submission

According to university Self-Employed guidelines, the following documentation package must be maintained for each pilot rupee:
1. **Bank Statement:** Official PDF bank statement with relevant incoming credit transactions highlighted.
2. **Transaction Reference / UTR:** Unique 12-digit UPI/UTR or bank transfer reference code recorded in Servora's database.
3. **Servora Commercial Service Receipt:** Formal receipt generated by the Servora system (`/my-bookings/<id>/receipt/`).
4. **Platform Ledger Entry:** Matched `RevenueTransaction` record in the database.
5. **Customer Confirmation & Review:** 1–5 star rating and feedback submitted on Servora marketplace.

## 6. How Commission Revenue is Calculated

- Formula: `Commission = Booking.total_amount × 10%`
- Computed using `DecimalField` calculations to avoid floating point inaccuracies.
- Example:
  - Total Booking: ₹3,500.00
  - Commission Rate: 10.00%
  - Servora Revenue: ₹350.00
  - Provider Net: ₹3,150.00
- **Idempotency Rule:** Commission records are guarded by a database `UniqueConstraint(fields=['revenue_type', 'booking'])`. Repeatedly marking a job complete will never generate duplicate revenue.

---

## 7. How Subscription Revenue is Calculated

- Plan: Servora Pro Tier at ₹399.00 per month.
- When an active provider initiates an upgrade, a `RevenueTransaction(revenue_type='subscription', amount=399.00)` is recorded.
- The provider receives 30 days of active Pro status with verified badges and top directory search priority.

---

## 8. How Featured Listing Revenue is Calculated

- Promotion: ₹99.00 for 3 days of prominent category placement.
- When a provider promotes a service, `RevenueTransaction(revenue_type='featured', amount=99.00)` is logged.
- The service displays a prominent "Featured" badge on homepage search feeds.

## 9. Demo Data vs. Actual Pilot Revenue

To maintain complete academic integrity, the Servora codebase strictly segregates synthetic demonstration data from genuine real-world pilot revenue:

| Dimension | Demonstration Data (`is_demo=True`) | Actual Pilot Data (`is_demo=False`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Origin** | Created via `python manage.py seed_data` | Created through real live usage |
| **Purpose** | Classroom demonstrations, test suites, UI showcase | Submission for university SCRGM evaluation |
| **Verification Status** | Tagged as `is_demo=True`, excluded from verified pool | Must be audited with `verification_status='verified'` |
| **SCRGM Target Tracking** | Strictly ₹0.00 contribution | Contributes 100% to verified progress tracker |

## 10. Administrative Verification Workflow

Admin/Staff users review transactions through `/platform/revenue/evidence/` or the Django Admin console:
1. **Initial State:** Real transactions are recorded with `verification_status='pending'`.
2. **Evidence Inspection:** Admin cross-checks bank account statement for matching UTR/transaction reference and date.
3. **Auditor Action:** Admin updates record:
   - `verification_status = 'verified'`
   - `has_evidence = True`
   - `evidence_note = "Confirmed against HDFC bank statement dated 10 Sep 2026, UTR: 425619283712"`
4. **Result:** Transaction is immediately credited to the live **Verified Revenue** counter.

## 12. Critical Limitations & Compliance Notice

> [!WARNING]
> **A Django Database Alone is NOT Proof of Actual Payment.**
> 
> The university SCRGM committee requires tangible external banking proof. Creating a database row or clicking a button in a web application does not constitute real-world revenue. 
> 
> 1. **No Fake GST Invoices:** Never issue tax invoices with fabricated GSTIN numbers. All Servora receipts are strictly "Commercial Service Receipts" between private parties.
> 2. **No Cash Payments:** Cash is categorically excluded by university self-employed regulations. Only documented bank/UPI transactions are valid.
> 3. **Non-Circular Funds:** Circular transactions (e.g. paying oneself or transferring funds back offline) violate university academic ethics and will result in disciplinary disqualification.
