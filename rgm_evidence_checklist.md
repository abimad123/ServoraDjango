# Servora — RGM Evidence Checklist & Compliance Guide

**University Model:** Student-Centric Revenue Generation Model (SCRGM)  
**Track:** Self-Employed Category  
**Target Revenue Bracket:** ₹10,000 – ₹24,999  
**Platform:** Servora Local Home Services Marketplace  

---

## 1. Official University Self-Employed Evidence Checklist

Before submitting your revenue pilot portfolio to the university evaluation committee, ensure every item on this checklist is thoroughly verified and documented:

- [ ] **1. Bank Account Statement:** Official bank account statement covering the exact active academic term period.
- [ ] **2. Highlighted Credit Entries:** All relevant incoming customer/provider payment credits clearly highlighted with highlighter or digital annotation.
- [ ] **3. Genuine Commercial Receipts:** Servora Commercial Service Receipts (`/my-bookings/<id>/receipt/`) printed or exported as PDF for each completed transaction.
- [ ] **4. Proof of Work Completed:** Evidence that the booked service actually occurred (e.g., job completion confirmation, before/after job notes, communication timestamps).
- [ ] **5. Customer Testimonials & Reviews:** Verified marketplace reviews from genuine customer accounts on Servora (`1–5 ★` with written comments).
- [ ] **6. Servora Platform Live Deployment / Portfolio:** Functional demonstration of the Servora platform showing registered customer and provider user accounts.
- [ ] **7. Application UI Screenshots:** Screenshots of the Platform Revenue Dashboard (`/platform/revenue/`), Evidence Audit Page (`/platform/revenue/evidence/`), and Booking Detail.
- [ ] **8. Social Media & Promotion Links (If applicable):** Verified Instagram/LinkedIn announcements, marketing flyers, or community referral posts.
- [ ] **9. Database RevenueTransaction Records:** Corresponding database entries exported via the administrative CSV export tool.
- [ ] **10. Booking Records & Lifecycles:** Audit log demonstrating the booking status transition from `pending` &rarr; `accepted` &rarr; `completed`.
- [ ] **11. Completed Service Evidence:** Timestamped booking record with verified service location address.
- [ ] **12. Unique Transaction References:** 12-digit UPI UTR numbers or bank IMPS/NEFT reference codes linked to each database transaction.
- [ ] **13. Executive Revenue Summary:** Clean executive summary table reconciling Gross Booking Value (GBV) against Servora 10% earned revenue.

## 2. University Compliance & Regulatory Rules

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### Critical SCRGM Submission Rules
> 1. **Cash Payments are Categorically Prohibited:** Under university SCRGM Self-Employed guidelines, cash transactions are **not acceptable**. All payments must be processed electronically via UPI, QR code, IMPS, NEFT, or debit/credit cards into a verifiable bank account.
> 2. **Current-Term Period Requirement:** All revenue transactions, bookings, and bank statement credits must fall strictly within the specified dates of the current academic evaluation term. Transactions from prior semesters are inadmissible.
> 3. **University Committee Verification:** The university scrutiny committee will conduct random sample checks, inspect original bank statements, and may directly contact customers or providers to verify that the service was genuinely rendered.
> 4. **Zero Tolerance for Fake Invoices:** Never issue tax invoices claiming GST, fake GSTIN numbers, or fake government registration. Servora receipts are strictly private commercial service receipts.
